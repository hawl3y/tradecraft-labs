The first interview of the practitioner research phase has been completed: a self-administered pilot interview (Interview #0), conducted to test and refine the interview guide before it is used with external participants.

## Purpose

Before approaching practitioners for interviews, it was important to validate that the interview guide actually surfaces the kind of evidence this research is looking for — rather than defaulting to generic descriptions of the role. Running the guide against a real, structured session (rather than reviewing it on paper) was the fastest way to find its weak points.

## What Changed

The pilot confirmed one methodological finding in particular: asking participants to walk through **real situations** produced substantially richer discussion of governance, trust, and decision-making than asking directly about those concepts. The interview guide has been revised to lead with situational/case-based prompts rather than abstract questions about the role.

## Preliminary Themes

With the standard anonymization applied to all practitioner data, several themes emerged that are consistent with — and in a couple of cases extend — the current working theories documented in the [Research Insights](/research/project-001-biso/artifacts/biso-research-insights) artifact:

- The role was consistently described in terms of **facilitating** decisions rather than making them.
- Trust was described not as agreement with cybersecurity decisions, but as confidence that stakeholders would be engaged early and kept informed through implementation.
- **Stakeholder preparation** — how a decision is introduced and rolled out — emerged as a distinct source of value, separate from the decision itself.
- During high-urgency events, the role's emphasis shifted from facilitating decisions toward supporting the business through implementation, a nuance worth tracking across future interviews.

Because this was a single, self-conducted pilot session, these observations are treated as preliminary. They will be weighed against the evidence gathered from external practitioner interviews rather than taken as confirmed findings on their own.

## Next Steps

With the guide validated and revised, outreach for the first external practitioner interviews begins next.
